Switching questions

Before you move.

What can you import for me?

Pretty much anything: client and vendor lists, existing events, budgets, task lists, timelines and templates. Send them during your trial in whatever format you have, whether that's a spreadsheet, an export or a PDF, and we'll bring them in so you don't have to rebuild anything by hand.

Do I have to move everything before my next event?

No. Plenty of planners finish their current events in the old tool and start new ones in Gatherwise. With templates, your run of show, tasks and checklists only have to be built once.

Do my clients need to create an account?

No. Clients sign in with a magic link, no password. Vendors can get exported timelines or layouts without logging in at all.

Is this only for weddings?

No. Planners also use it for corporate events, galas, showers and social events.

What happens when the trial ends?

The trial runs for 14 days on Professional. After that, pick the plan that fits. There's no free tier, and you can cancel any month.
