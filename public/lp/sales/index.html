Questions

Before you try it.

Can I keep using my own website?

Yes. You add the Gatherwise inquiry form to the site you already have, and new inquiries come into Gatherwise as leads.

Can I bring my existing clients over?

Yes. During your trial, send us your client and vendor lists in whatever format you have and we'll import them. You can also add clients as each event starts.

How many questionnaires do I get?

Starter includes 1, Professional includes 3, and Elite is unlimited. The 14-day trial runs on Professional.

Is this only for weddings?

No. Planners use it for corporate events, galas, showers and social events too.
